## Current direction

Dimensional paper cutouts, faded indigo and ivory, calm open ocean, and gently deforming water/cloud forms. Wildlife and environmental behavior should be believable. The desired mood is wonder and solitude with occasional danger.

Ichigo is a young child with unspecified age and gender, wearing their dad's very oversized number 15 sports jersey, with no hat. Their vessel is a wooden bucket that is giant relative to them. Earlier art depicts an adult in a skiff; use it only for environment, material, and palette reference.

Inventory, expanded weather, and natural time-of-day transitions are future work. Godot with typed GDScript is the proposed engine stack, pending the visual experiment on the target Mac.

This directory is the working source for concept documents and art. Files previously delivered in the chat's sibling `outputs` directory are snapshots.
