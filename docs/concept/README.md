# Ichigo concept design

The [integrated design package](../../documents/design/README.md) is the current planning authority. This folder preserves earlier concepts and art studies. Its original survival, navigation, and camera proposals are superseded where the integrated plan differs.

The current phase is visual exploration. No playable build exists yet.

Use as little in-game text as possible. Communicate story, mechanics, and wildlife learning through visuals, sound, behavior, and interaction, with concise text where needed for clarity or accessibility.

## Current direction

Dimensional paper cutouts, faded indigo and ivory, calm open ocean, and gently deforming water/cloud forms. Wildlife and environmental behavior should be believable. The desired mood is wonder and solitude with occasional danger.

Ichigo is a young child with unspecified age and gender, wearing their dad's very oversized number 15 sports jersey, with no hat. Their vessel is a wooden bucket that is giant relative to them. Earlier art depicts an adult in a skiff; use it only for environment, material, and palette reference.

Inventory, fishing, weather/time, encounters, and story now have dedicated plans in the current package. Implementation begins with the camera and asset-cohesion experiments, including paper, low-poly, and medium-poly candidates. Godot with typed GDScript is the baseline stack, pending the visual experiment on the target Mac.

This directory contains the source concept history and art studies. Current planning documents live in `documents/design`. Files previously delivered in the chat's `outputs` directory are snapshots.
